Empanadas de Queso

I picked this recipe because they are relatively easy to make and are very delicious.
This specific recipe I chose is a traditional Bolivian recipe that is a favorite in my family.

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • Dough
    • 3 cups of flour
    • 1/2 cup of butter
    • 1/4 teaspoon of salt
    • 3 teaspoons of baking powder
    • 3 egg yolks
    • 1 cup of warm milk (or water)

  • Filling
    • 4 cups of grated monterrey jack cheese (if you can get queso fresco use that)
    • 3 eggs whites
    • 1 egg

Steps

  • Dough
    1. In a food processor, mix the flour, baking powder and salt.

    2. Add the butter and pulse until the flour looks like sand.

    3. Add the eggs yolks and slowly add the milk until the dough is soft.

    4. Put the dough on the table and work with your hands until dough is soft but not wet.

    5. Divide the dough in 5 portions and wrap them in plastic and set them aside for 1 hour.

  • Filling
    1. Whisk the egg whites until they are hard.

    2. Add the cheese to this mix just before you are making the empanadas,
      otherwise the egg whites will lose their foamy consistency.

  • Finishing
    1. Divide each ball of dough in 4 pieces for a total of 20 little balls.

    2. Cover them with plastic and let them rest for 5 minutes.

    3. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees Farenheit.

    4. Work one empanada at a time.

    5. Roll one ball at a time into a flat round oval.

    6. Put the filling on one far side of the rolled dough, around 1 tablespoon

    7. Close the empanada by folding the empty half over the filling.

    8. Place the empanadas on a baking sheet that is covered with parchment paper.

    9. Whisk the other egg and, using a brush, paint each empanada with the whisked egg. Make sure you brush the entire empanada.

    10. Bake them for 20 min or until well brown.

    11. Serve warm and enjoy!

Notes

  • Information
    • Will take about 2 hours
    • Serves 20 empanadas
    • Average cost per batch: $12.00

  • Materials
    • Food Processor
    • Mixing Bowls
    • Whisk
    • Measuring cups/spoons
    • Plastic wrap
    • Rolling pin

    • Baking sheet

    • Pastry brush
