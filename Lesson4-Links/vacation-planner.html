My Dream Trip to Puerto Rico

Ultimate Travel Guide

Trip Research

I want to visit Puerto Rico because it has beautiful beaches and rich culture.
You can find more information on Trip Advisor and you can also check the Weather.
Hurricane season is usually in September, so it would be best to plan a trip in the spring or summer.

Getting There and Staying There

Flight and Hotel Booking

There are many different ways to book your flight to Puerto Rico, and you can plan it out
in advance using Expedia. For hotels, you could check Booking.com for great places to stay.
I would visit Playa Escondida or Viejo San Juan because they are both beautiful and have a lot of history.

Transportation Info

There are many different manners of local transportation to get around Puerto Rico.
You could take a bus or a train to get around the island, and you can even rent a car and
travel around the whole island in under a day.
To not get lost, I reccomend using Google Maps to help you find your way around.
